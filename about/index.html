About Eastern Orchid

Sharing the beauty of Chinese orchids — their culture, their stories, and the joy of growing them.

Our Mission

Eastern Orchid is a personal website created by a Chinese orchid enthusiast. Here, I document my daily orchid life, share the rich cultural heritage of Chinese orchids, and celebrate the quiet joy of watching orchids bloom.

This is not a commercial site — it is a genuine orchid lover's garden diary. Through these pages, I hope to introduce the world to the unique beauty of Chinese orchids: not just the vibrant Phalaenopsis, but the subtle elegance and fragrance of traditional Chinese cymbidiums.
