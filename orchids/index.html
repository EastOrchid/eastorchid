Orchid Origins

China is one of the original homes of orchids. Wild orchids grow across the Yangtze River basin and southern mountain regions. For over two thousand years, the Chinese have cultivated and cherished these elegant plants.

Orchid Species

The main Chinese orchid species include: Cymbidium goeringii (Spring Orchid), C. ensifolium (Jian Orchid), C. faberi (Hui Orchid), C. sinense (Mo Orchid), and C. kanran (Cold Orchid). Each has its unique character and blooming season.
