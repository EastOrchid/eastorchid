Garden Life

Growing Chinese Orchids with Patience and Tradition

About Our Garden

Our orchid garden is a living space where Chinese cymbidiums are grown, observed and appreciated through the seasons. Sheltered from harsh winds yet open to natural light and fresh air, it provides the ideal environment for traditional Chinese orchids to thrive — from the first spring blooms to the quiet dormancy of winter.

Orchid Collection

Five traditional Chinese cymbidium species — each with its own character, fragrance, and growing story.

Chunlan

Cymbidium goeringii

Spring orchid — the most beloved Chinese orchid, treasured for its delicate fragrance and elegant form.

Huilan

Cymbidium faberi

Noble orchid with tall flower stalks and multiple blossoms — a classic in Chinese gardens.

Jianlan

Cymbidium ensifolium

Summer orchid — blooms when the weather warms, filling the garden with its gentle fragrance.

Hanlan

Cymbidium kanran

Cold orchid — blooms in winter, admired for its graceful flowers and refined scent.

Molan

Cymbidium sinense

Ink orchid — named for its deep purple flowers, a favourite among serious orchid collectors.

Seasonal Garden

Each season brings a different rhythm to the orchid garden — from spring blooms to winter rest.

Spring

Chunlan blooming season

Spring is the season of awakening. Chunlan flowers open with a delicate fragrance, and new growth emerges from the compost. This is the most anticipated time in the orchid garden.

Summer

Jianlan growth and care

Warm weather brings strong growth. Jianlan sends up flower spikes, and the garden is at its most lush. Daily watering and careful attention to light levels keep the orchids thriving.

Autumn

Orchid maintenance

A time for repotting, dividing established clumps, and preparing the garden for cooler months. Autumn is when the gardener's patience is rewarded with strong root systems.

Winter

Protecting orchids in cold days

Winter is quiet but not empty. Hanlan orchids bloom in the cold, and the garden rests. Protection from frost and careful watering keep the collection healthy until spring.
